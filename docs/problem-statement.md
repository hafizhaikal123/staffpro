# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Organizations, HR staff, managers and employees who need to manage employee information and workplace records.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Managing employee details, attendance, leave records and other workplace activities can become difficult when the information is kept separately or updated manually.

## Why does it matter?
It can take more time to find and update records and information may become outdated or inconsistent.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Managing employee information, attendance, leave records and other workplace activities can become difficult when records are kept separately. StaffPro aims to provide one system to help organize and manage these records more easily.
